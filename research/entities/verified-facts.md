# Verified Facts Ledger

Last updated: 2026-09-29

This is the current fact layer for building pages. “Verified” means supported by the source inventory; it does not imply the dataset is complete.

## Game / multiplayer

- Genre: action RPG.
- Up to four players.
- Couch co-op and online play are supported.
- Developer demo states local couch groups can participate in online multiplayer with an available slot.
- The sequel uses a large interconnected world rather than only the first game's isolated mission-table flow.

## Gear / progression

- Armor is split into Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings and Boots.
- Talismans are a new item category and provide passive playstyle bonuses.
- Some talismans gain experience/strength.
- Tasty Bone is shown/described as granting a wolf companion.
- Gear may have random effects.
- Blacksmith can improve lower item-power gear.
- Echo Shards can reroll gear effects.
- Enchantment books are collected separately and applied to gear, a change from the first game.
- Inner Mines: weapon attacks deploy two mines that explode when enemies approach.
- Jump attacks are new.
- Firework Arrows return.

## World / exploration

- World is interconnected.
- Side quests are represented on the map.
- Procedurally generated dungeons exist.
- A Guiding Line assists navigation.
- Echo Shards can be found through procedural-dungeon activity.
- Soul-Corrupted mobs can drop Echo Shards.

## Co-op UX

- Mini Inventory allows players to manage gear without the first game's turn-taking bottleneck.
- Demonstrated controller shortcut: D-pad Up.
- Multiple players reviving together increases revive speed.

## Item / artifact-like evidence

- Creeper Candle can be placed and explodes after a delay, damaging enemies around it.
- Exact item taxonomy, cooldown and acquisition are not yet verified.

## Boss / story

### Copper Monstrosity
- Story boss.
- Encountered while the party is searching for Supreme Evoker.
- After the fight, a Note Block is picked up.
- Developers explicitly call that Note Block important to the story.

### Twisted Warden
- Officially revealed entity/boss candidate; detailed attacks/loot require gameplay evidence before publication.

### Illager High Council
Official material identifies:
- Prime Enchanter
- Grand Illusioner
- Supreme Evoker
